Rewrite one step of a reasoning chain as a single short sentence that states exactly what the step claims.

Rules:
1. Keep every fact, name, number and date the step states. Do not correct anything, even if the step is wrong.
2. Drop only words that carry no claim: "Therefore", "So", "Thus", step numbers, references to other steps.
3. If the step states two claims, keep both in one sentence.
4. Keep hedges such as "likely" or "about" if the step has them.
5. No explanation, no extra sentence.

Example
Step: "Therefore, since bananas are rich in potassium, eating one would add potassium to the diet."
Claim: Bananas are rich in potassium, so eating one adds potassium to the diet.

Example
Step: "The Eiffel Tower was completed in 1889."
Claim: The Eiffel Tower was completed in 1889.
